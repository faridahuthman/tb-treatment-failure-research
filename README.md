# Tuberculosis Treatment Failure Research

## About the Project

This repository documents my undergraduate research journey investigating factors associated with tuberculosis (TB) treatment failure among TB patients attending TB clinics in Kwara State, Nigeria.

The repository captures the different stages of the research process, from proposal development and study design to data management, statistical analysis, interpretation of findings, and preparation of the final research report.

This project reflects my academic interests in Medical Laboratory Science, tuberculosis research, public health, and evidence-based healthcare.

## Research Objectives

The study aims to investigate factors associated with TB treatment failure, with attention to relevant sociodemographic, clinical, treatment-related, and healthcare access factors.

## Research Journey

The repository is organised to document the following stages:

- **Proposal:** Initial research idea, background, problem statement, objectives, and study justification.
- **Literature Review:** Relevant published studies and supporting evidence.
- **Methodology:** Study design, study population, sampling approach, and data collection methods.
- **Data Management:** Data organisation, coding, cleaning, and preparation for analysis.
- **Data Analysis:** Statistical analysis and presentation of findings.
- **Results and Discussion:** Interpretation of findings in relation to existing literature.
- **Final Report:** The completed undergraduate research report.

## Tools and Technologies

- Microsoft Excel — data organisation and management.
- IBM SPSS Statistics — statistical analysis.
- Microsoft Word — research writing and documentation.
- GitHub — versioning and documentation of the research process.

## Research Ethics and Data Privacy

This repository is intended for academic documentation and learning. Confidential patient information, personally identifiable data, and restricted research materials will not be published.

Any shared datasets or supporting materials will be anonymised and included only where permitted by applicable ethical approvals and data-sharing requirements.
