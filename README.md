## Tuberculosis Treatment Failure Research

## About the Project

This repository documents my undergraduate research journey investigating factors associated with tuberculosis (TB) treatment failure among TB patients attending TB clinics in Kwara State, Nigeria.

It captures the progression of the research from the initial proposal to the completed research project, bringing together the study's background, literature review, methodology, results, discussion, and conclusions in a single final report.

## Research Focus

The study investigates factors associated with TB treatment failure, with attention to sociodemographic, clinical, treatment-related, and healthcare access factors.

## Research Documents

- **Research Proposal:** The initial research plan, including the background, problem statement, objectives, and proposed methodology.
- **Final Research Report:** The complete undergraduate project, containing the literature review, methodology, results, discussion, conclusions, and references.

Additional research materials may be included as they become available and where sharing is permitted.
